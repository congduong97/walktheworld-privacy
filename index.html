Privacy Policy

Walk The World · Last updated: September 27, 2026

Walk The World ("we", "our", or "the app") helps you walk biblical journeys while tracking your activity. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, display name, and password (stored securely by Firebase Authentication).
  • Profile settings: timezone, daily step goal, stride length, Bible version preference, notification preferences, and privacy settings.
  • Location data: GPS coordinates during an active walk to record your route, distance, and journey progress. With your permission, location may be collected in the background while a walk is in progress.
  • Activity and fitness data: steps, walk duration, distance, pace, streaks, and journey progress.
  • User content: journal entries and reflections you choose to save (if you use those features).
  • Social features: friend connections and activity shared according to your visibility settings.

2. How we use your information

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Track walks, routes, steps, and journey progress.
  • Display scripture and audio content during your journey.
  • Send reminders if you enable notifications.
  • Improve app reliability and fix issues.

3. Third-party services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database, and app infrastructure. See Firebase Privacy.
  • Bible Gateway — scripture text and audio loaded in an in-app browser during reading or listening. Bible Gateway has its own privacy practices when you interact with their content.
  • Apple Maps — map display during walks on iOS.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers needed to run the app (such as Firebase), when you choose to share activity with friends, or when required by law.

5. Data retention

We keep your data while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law.

6. Your choices

  • You can deny or revoke location and motion permissions in your device Settings (some features may not work without them).
  • You can update profile and privacy settings in the app.
  • You can sign out at any time.
  • You can request account deletion from within the app or by contacting us (see below).

7. Children

Walk The World is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures including encrypted connections and Firebase security rules. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and update the "Last updated" date.
